Privacy policy

Effective 27 August 2026

The short version: SeedCairn doesn't collect anything. There's no account, no server, and nothing about your recovery phrase, its shares, or the people you name ever leaves your device. This website doesn't use cookies or run analytics either.

The app

SeedCairn has no server and no account system. Everything the app does — reading your recovery phrase, splitting it into shares, naming the people you trust, writing shares to NFC cards or printing them — happens entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere except locally, on that device.

The only thing the app remembers between sessions is whether you've completed the onboarding introduction, so it doesn't show it to you again. That's stored locally on your device (using standard on-device storage) and is never sent anywhere — it doesn't relate to your recovery phrase or shares in any way.

Permissions

On Android, the app requests the NFC permission so it can write shares to, and read shares from, NFC cards you tap against your device. This happens entirely locally: no data is sent over the network as part of using NFC.

This website

This website (seedcairn.com) sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind. We don't know who visits, and we don't want to.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which means your browser makes a direct request to Google's servers to fetch font files — as with any request to a third-party server, your IP address is visible to Google as part of that request, in the same way it would be visiting any website that links to an external resource. Google Fonts, used this way, does not set cookies. See Google Fonts' own privacy FAQ for details of how they handle this.

Third parties

If you download the app from the App Store or Google Play, that download is subject to Apple's or Google's own privacy policy, not this one. The same applies if you view SeedCairn's source code on GitHub.

Children's privacy

SeedCairn isn't directed at children, but since the app and this website don't collect any data from anyone regardless of age, there's nothing further to say here.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Since the app doesn't collect anything, changes are expected to be rare and mostly about clarity rather than substance.
