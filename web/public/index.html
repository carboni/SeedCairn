keep it together

Keep something precious with the people you trust.

Your crypto wallet has a recovery phrase. Lose it and your money is gone forever. Write it down in one place and any fire, flood or nosy visitor can take everything.

SeedCairn splits that phrase into five pieces and helps you leave one with each of five people you trust. Any three of them can bring it back. Fewer than three is useless to anyone.

Five pieces. Any three bring it back.

It takes about ten minutes, once. Then your backup is out in the world, in the hands of people who care about you.

STEP ONE

Type in your phrase

The twelve or twenty-four words your wallet gave you. Words are checked as you type, and each one stays hidden unless you hold your finger on it.

STEP TWO

Name five people

A sibling, a best friend, a solicitor, a safe at your parents' house. Each piece carries a name, so future-you knows exactly where to go.

STEP THREE

Put them on something real

Tap each piece onto a blank NFC card, print it, or stamp it into metal. Then hand them out. Nothing is ever emailed or sent.

Losing a piece is fine

Two people can move house, fall out with you, or lose their card entirely. Three is still enough. That is the whole point of splitting it.

One piece reveals nothing

Anyone holding a single card learns nothing at all about your phrase — not a word of it, not even a hint. They would need two others to conspire with.

Questions people ask

Do you ever see my recovery phrase? No. There is no account and no server to send it to. Everything happens on your own device, and you can turn the internet off while you do it.
What if one of my five people looks at their card? They learn nothing. A single piece gives away no part of your phrase. Three of the five would have to get together and try.
What if I lose my phone? Your backup is not on your phone — it is out with your five people. Install the app anywhere, gather three pieces, and your phrase comes back.
Do the people holding a piece need the app? Only if they help you recover. Day to day they just keep the card safe. When you need it, they hand it back or tap it on your phone.
Is this only for Bitcoin? It works with any wallet that gave you a twelve or twenty-four word phrase, which is nearly all of them.
