SeedCairn

keep it together

Instead of one seed phrase written on one piece of paper, split it into five pieces and give one to each person you trust. No single piece reveals anything on its own; any three together rebuild it exactly. Nothing ever leaves your device — no accounts, no server, no stored state.

How it works

Shamir's Secret Sharing, in four steps

SeedCairn uses SLIP-39, the open standard for splitting a wallet seed into shares — the same math a security team would use, not a proprietary scheme.

Enter your phrase

Type in the BIP-39 seed phrase you want to back up. It never leaves the device.

Split into shares

SeedCairn splits it into five SLIP-39 shares using Shamir's Secret Sharing.

Give one to each person

Name five people you trust. Write, print, or stamp one share for each of them.

Any three rebuild it

Bring any three shares back together to recover the exact original phrase.

Why SeedCairn

Built to be trusted, not just used

◆

Nothing leaves your device

No accounts, no server, no analytics on your phrase or shares. What you enter stays on the device you enter it on.

◆

Works offline

Split and recover a phrase with no network connection at all — put the device in airplane mode first if you want to be sure.

◆

Open standard

Built on SLIP-39 and BIP-39, not a proprietary format — shares stay recoverable with other compatible tools, not just SeedCairn.

◆

Write it, print it, stamp it

Export shares to NFC cards, paper, or metal — durable, offline storage for the people holding them.

Status

Early and honest about it

SeedCairn is in active development.

The SLIP-39 splitting and recovery logic is implemented from spec and tested against all 45 official test vectors — but it isn't wired into the app yet, and the app itself hasn't shipped. Don't use this for a real seed phrase yet.

Source is published for verification at github.com/carboni/SeedCairn — you're welcome to read it, clone it, and build your own copy. It isn't open source for redistribution; see the repository's LICENSE.
